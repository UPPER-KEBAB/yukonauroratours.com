What does an aurora inquiry include?＋

Send dates, party size, pickup point, warmth needs, mobility notes and photo goals. WH-MICRO routes the quote from those details and keeps the next step inside the inquiry.

What do Yukon aurora tours usually cost?＋

Typical shared Whitehorse aurora outings run CAD 159–399 per person, with private, photo-focused and multi-night requests pricing higher. Quotes for your dates come through the inquiry.

Can I request photo-focused or private aurora viewing?＋

Yes. Select the premium photo night or multi-night variant for tripod space, photo coaching, private pace, larger parties or multiple viewing windows.
