Common Questions

When is the best time to see the aurora?

Aurora season runs from late August through early April. Peak months are November through March, with long clear nights and frequent solar activity.

Will I definitely see the Northern Lights?

We cannot guarantee sightings — nature is unpredictable. However, we choose nights with the highest forecast probability and have strong success rates across the season.

How long do tours last?

Standard tours run 4 hours (typically 9pm to 1am). Photography-focused tours may run longer.

What should I wear?

Dress for extreme cold: insulated boots, down jacket, snow pants, mittens, hat, and face protection. Temperatures can drop below -30C on clear nights.

What if the weather is bad?

Cloud cover blocks aurora visibility. If conditions are unsuitable, we offer reschedule or full refund. You will not pay for a night with no chance of seeing anything.

Can children come on tours?

Children are welcome with adult supervision. The experience is best for ages 8+ who can handle the late hours and cold. Contact us for family arrangements.
