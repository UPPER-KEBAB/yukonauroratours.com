About the night
More questions ↗
Is an aurora sighting guaranteed?＋
Do not treat a tour booking as a guarantee. Ask how the operator describes viewing uncertainty and what, if anything, their written policy offers after an unsuccessful outing.
Should I book more than one night?＋
Compare flexibility, your budget and other plans. Extra nights create more opportunities to go out but do not guarantee a sighting.
What camera should I bring?＋
Ask the provider what equipment their photography guidance supports. Confirm tripod access and charging arrangements before choosing what to carry.
