Our Aurora Tours

Our aurora tours are designed for maximum viewing success. We monitor solar activity, cloud cover, and weather patterns nightly to choose the best locations. Tours depart after dark from Whitehorse and travel to viewing sites with minimal light pollution. All tours include a knowledgeable guide who explains the science and folklore of the Northern Lights.

What's Included

  • Expert aurora guides with years of local viewing experience
  • Prime viewing locations away from Whitehill city glow
  • Real-time aurora and weather forecast monitoring
  • Heated viewing areas or vehicle-based viewing options
  • Hot drinks and snacks provided
  • Small group sizes for a personal experience
  • Photography assistance available on request
